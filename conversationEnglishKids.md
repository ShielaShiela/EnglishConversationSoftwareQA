# 🌟 English Speaking Adventure

### 20-Lesson Conversational English Program for Kids

**Age:** 11 years old
**Level:** Basic English
**Lesson Length:** 25 minutes
**Main Focus:** Listening • Speaking • Confidence • Everyday English
**Future Goal:** Build enough English confidence to transition into **Coding in English** 💻

---

## 🎯 Our Learning Goal

The goal of this program is to help the student become **more comfortable listening to and speaking English** through short, interactive, and enjoyable lessons.

Rather than focusing heavily on grammar or memorization, lessons will encourage the student to:

* 👂 Understand simple spoken English
* 💬 Respond to everyday questions
* 🗣️ Speak using complete but simple sentences
* 🎧 Follow short English instructions
* 🌱 Gradually become more confident speaking independently
* 💻 Learn useful English that can later be used in Scratch, Roblox, and programming lessons

> **Our approach:** Start simple → Build confidence → Speak more → Follow instructions → Get ready for coding!

---

# ⏱️ What Happens in a 25-Minute Class?

Each lesson is divided into short activities to help maintain attention and keep the class moving.

| Time          | Activity         | What We Do                                                     |
| ------------- | ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 👋 **3 min**  | Hello & Warm-Up  | Simple conversation about his day, mood, school, or interests  |
| 🖼️ **4 min** | Words of the Day | Learn 4–6 useful words with pictures and examples              |
| 👂 **5 min**  | Listen & Speak   | Listen, repeat, answer, and practice short sentence patterns   |
| 💬 **5 min**  | Let's Talk!      | Guided conversation using today's topic                        |
| 🎮 **5 min**  | Game / Challenge | Guessing games, picture challenges, choices, or mini role-play |
| ⭐ **3 min**   | Review & Goodbye | Review new words and finish with a small speaking challenge    |

**Activities change every few minutes**, so the lesson stays active and engaging.

# 🗺️ Our 20-Lesson Learning Journey

## 🌱 LEVEL 1 — Let's Start Talking!

### Lessons 1–5 • Building Comfort & Confidence

**Goal:** Become comfortable speaking with the teacher and answering simple questions.

### 👋 Lesson 1 — All About Me

**Let's learn to:** Introduce ourselves.

💬 *“My name is…”*
💬 *“I am 11 years old.”*
💬 *“My favorite ___ is…”*

**Words:** name • age • school • favorite • like

🎮 **Challenge:** My Favorites Game

---

### 🎮 Lesson 2 — Things I Like

**Let's learn to:** Talk about things we like and don't like.

💬 *“I like…”*
💬 *“I don't like…”*
💬 *“Do you like ___?”*

**Topics:** games • food • sports • hobbies

🎮 **Challenge:** Like 👍 or Don't Like 👎

---

### 👨‍👩‍👦 Lesson 3 — My Family

**Let's learn to:** Introduce and describe family members.

💬 *“This is my…”*
💬 *“He/She likes…”*
💬 *“I have…”*

**Words:** mother • father • brother • sister • family

🎮 **Challenge:** Who Is It?

---

### ⏰ Lesson 4 — My Day

**Let's learn to:** Talk about our daily routine.

💬 *“I wake up at…”*
💬 *“I go to school.”*
💬 *“After school, I…”*

**Words:** wake up • eat • go • study • play • sleep

🎮 **Challenge:** Put My Day in Order

---

### 🕹️ Lesson 5 — My Favorite Games

**Let's learn to:** Talk about games and explain what we enjoy.

💬 *“My favorite game is…”*
💬 *“I like this game because…”*
💬 *“My favorite character is…”*

**Words:** game • player • character • play • win • lose

🎮 **Challenge:** Guess My Game!

---

**Goal:** Use English to ask questions, follow instructions, solve problems, and communicate ideas.

**Let's learn to:** Ask questions to discover something.

💬 *“Is it big?”*
💬 *“Can it fly?”*
💬 *“Does it have ___?”*

### 📖 Lesson 15 — Tell Me a Story!

# 💻 LEVEL 4 — English Meets Coding!

### Lessons 16–20 • Preparing for Coding in English

**Goal:** Become familiar with English instructions and vocabulary commonly used during beginner coding lessons.

### 🖱️ Lesson 16 — Computer English

**Let's learn to:** Understand common computer instructions.

🖱️ click
⌨️ type
📂 open
❌ close
👆 drag
🔍 find

### 🤖 Lesson 17 — Be the Robot!

**Let's learn to:** Give clear instructions.

💬 *“Move forward.”*
💬 *“Turn right.”*
💬 *“Move three steps.”*
💬 *“Repeat!”*

🤖 **Challenge:** Program the Teacher/Robot

### 🎮 Lesson 18 — Build a Game with Words

**Let's learn to:** Describe how a simple game works.

**Words:** start • move • jump • touch • score • win

💬 *“The character moves.”*

### 🔀 Lesson 19 — If This Happens...

**Let's learn to:** Understand simple cause and effect.

💬 **IF** I touch the coin → **THEN** I get a point.

**Words:** if • then • when • because

🎮 **Challenge:** What Happens Next?

---

### 🏆 Lesson 20 — English + Coding Challenge!

**Let's use everything we've learned!**

The student will:

👂 Listen to English instructions
🗣️ Answer questions
🤖 Give commands
🖱️ Understand computer vocabulary
🎮 Complete a small coding-style challenge

### ⭐ Final Mission

**Can I follow enough English to start learning coding in English?**

At the end of the lesson, we can decide whether the student is ready to begin a beginner **Scratch + English** learning program.

# 🌟 How Speaking Confidence Will Grow

We don't expect long answers immediately.

### Step 1

👩‍🏫 **Teacher:** Do you like Roblox?
👦 **Student:** Yes.

### Step 2

👦 **Student:** Yes, I do.

### Step 3

👦 **Student:** Yes, I do. I like Roblox.

### Step 4

👦 **Student:** Yes, I do. I like Roblox because I can play with my friends.

✨ **Small answers gradually become real conversations.**

# 💡 Teaching Approach

### 🖼️ Visual First

Pictures, objects, characters, and demonstrations help the student understand English without needing long explanations.

### 🎮 Learn Through Games

Short challenges, guessing games, role-play, and interactive activities keep lessons engaging.

### 💬 Speaking Over Memorizing

The student will be encouraged to **use** new English instead of only memorizing vocabulary.

### 🌱 Gentle Correction

Mistakes are corrected naturally without constantly interrupting the conversation.

For example:

**Student:** *“I like play Roblox.”*
**Teacher:** *“You like playing Roblox! What do you like to do in Roblox?”*

### 🧩 Adjusted to the Student

The 20 lessons provide a learning direction, but the pace and difficulty can be adjusted based on the student's progress, interests, and attention during class.
