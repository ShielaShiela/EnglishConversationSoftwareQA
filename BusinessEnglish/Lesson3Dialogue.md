# Lesson 3: Workday Routine — Sample Conversations

---

## Conversation 1 — Morning Greeting at the Office

**S:** Good morning, David! How was your **commute** today?

**D:** Morning, Sara! Not bad, actually. The train was on time for once. How about you?

**S:** Lucky you! Mine was a bit slow — there was traffic. Anyway, do you have a minute to **catch up** before the meeting?

**D:** Sure! Let me just **log in** and check my emails first. Give me five minutes.

**S:** No problem. I'll grab us both a coffee.

---

## Conversation 2 — Discussing the Day's Tasks

**M:** Hey Lily, what does your **schedule** look like today?

**L:** Pretty busy. I need to **prioritize** the client report — I have a **deadline** this afternoon.

**M:** Oh, that's soon! Are you **on track**?

**L:** I think so. I just need to **wrap up** the last section. My manager will **check in** with me at 3 PM.

**M:** Sounds good. Let me know if you need help reviewing anything.

**L:** Thanks so much, I really appreciate that!

---

## Conversation 3 — End of the Workday

**R:** Almost done for the day! Did you manage to **wrap up** everything?

**J:** Almost. I just have to send one more email and then I can **log out**.

**R:** I know that feeling! Did you **catch up** on everything from yesterday?

**J:** Yes, finally! My morning was hectic — back-to-back meetings. But things are **on track** now.

**R:** Great! Have a good evening. Don't forget we have an early meeting tomorrow.

**J:** Oh right — 8 AM. I'll set my alarm early. See you then!

---

> Tip: Key vocabulary words are highlighted in bold. Try reading each 
> conversation aloud with a partner!
